## Description [4000]
Who's the best point guard in the league? Don't argue. Rank it.

Floor 2 Rafters is fan-voted basketball rankings. Pick between two players at a time, head to head, and your top 10 builds itself, one tap at a time. Fill every position, and your overall top 50 builds itself from your lists.

THE FANS DECIDE, LIVE
Every fan's list adds up into the Fan Board, live on the Jumbotron. Every pick counts the moment you make it. See who rose and fell this week, and where your list disagrees with everyone else.

RANK THE GREATS
A separate All-Time game: Jordan or LeBron? Kareem or Russell? Compare career stats, titles, MVPs, All-Star and All-NBA selections, then build your All-Time top 10.

SETTLE THE GROUP CHAT
Start a private group, drop the link in the chat, and get your own live board: same scoring as the Fan Board, just your people. See everyone's #1.

NEVER MISS A MOVE
An alert when your #1 climbs or slides, plus a weekly recap.

YOUR LIST, YOUR WAY
• Head-to-head picks at PG, SG, SF, PF and C
• Keep going past 10, all the way to 50
• Re-check any list head to head whenever the season changes your mind
• Hot Take tags when you're 5+ spots off the fans
• Share your top 10 as an image

Free. No ads in the app. Sign in with your email to save your lists and count on the Fan Board.

Floor 2 Rafters is a fan project and isn't affiliated with the NBA or any team. Stats: regular season, labeled with their season.

## App Privacy (Apple "nutrition label") / Data safety (Google)
Data collected, all **linked to the user**, **not used for tracking**, used for **App Functionality** only:
- Contact info: **Email address** (sign-in)
- User content: **Other user content** (your ranked lists, feedback messages)
- Identifiers: **User ID** (your account id)
- Group nickname (shown only to people in the same group): **Other user content**
Not collected: location, contacts, photos, health, purchases, browsing history, device ID, ads data.
Google Data safety: data is encrypted in transit; users can delete their account and data in the app (Me → Settings → Delete my account).

## Review notes (paste into "Notes for review")
Fans sign in with an email code. For review, use this account: type the email, tap the button, and a password box appears.
- Email: review@floor2rafters.com (password sign-in, only for this address: create it in Supabase → Authentication → Users → Add user, with a password)
- Password: the one you set for it
- The app works without signing in too (picks save on the phone).
Account deletion: Me → Settings → Delete my account and lists.

What the app does beyond the website: phone alerts for the weekly board (asked after sign-in), the native share sheet for share images, invite links (floor2rafters.com/join/…) that open the app on the join screen, haptic feedback on every pick, and built-in libraries so it opens without a connection. Friend groups: Board → My Groups (the review account is in a sample group). No purchases in the app.

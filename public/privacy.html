Privacy

Last updated October 9, 2026

Floor 2 Rafters ("F2R", "we") is a fan-voted NBA rankings site. This page explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

Your sign-in. Your email address, or the basic profile Google shares if you sign in with Google (name, email, profile photo). We use it to sign you in and to send your sign-in links.

Your picks. Your player lists and progress (like XP, streaks and your head-to-head answers). We combine everyone's lists into the public fan rankings. Your individual lists are never shown to other people with your name or email.

Your settings. The display name you choose (shown on Me and on share images you make) and your notification choices.

Friend groups. If you start or join a group, the other people in that group see the name you pick for the group and your #1 player on each list, and the group board combines the group's lists. People outside the group see nothing. Leave a group any time and you drop off its board right away.

Feedback you send. When you use Send feedback, we keep your message, the topic you picked, which screen you were on and your browser type, plus your account if you're signed in, so we can follow up and fix things.

On your device. If you pick without signing in, your lists are saved only in your browser on that device. When you sign in, they move to your account. The app also remembers small things on your device, like which tab you were on and whether you've seen the welcome screen.

Basic technical data. Our hosting, database and email providers keep standard logs (like IP address and browser type) to run and secure the service.

What we don't do

We don't sell your personal information. We don't post anything on your behalf. Share images are made on your phone and only go where you send them. We don't send marketing email unless you choose to sign up for it.

Notifications

You choose which updates you want in Settings (weekly recap, your player moved, streak reminder). The weekly recap comes by email to your sign-in address, with a one-tap unsubscribe link in every email. In the phone app, updates come as phone alerts once you allow them; to send those we store a device token from Apple or Google for your phone, and delete it when you sign out. You can turn each update off in Settings at any time.

Who helps us run F2R

Supabase (database and sign-in), Vercel (hosting), Resend (sign-in and weekly recap emails), Apple Push Notification service and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (phone alerts), and Stripe (F2R Pro payments). If you use Google sign-in, Google handles that step under its own privacy policy.

F2R Pro payments

If you buy F2R Pro, Stripe handles the payment. We never see or store your card number; we keep your Pro status, plan, renewal date and Stripe's customer and subscription ids so we know your Pro is active.

Ads

The website may show ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to show and measure ads; you can control personalized ads at adssettings.google.com. There are no ads in the phone apps or for F2R Pro members, and we don't share your lists, email or account with advertisers.

Deleting your account

Settings → Delete my account and lists removes your sign-in, lists, progress and settings right away. You can also email us and we'll do it. You're also removed from any groups. Feedback you sent stays, but is no longer linked to you. Combined weekly rankings that were already published don't identify you and stay up.

Kids

F2R isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change what we collect, we'll update this page and the date above.
